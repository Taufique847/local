# M1 — PROJECT FOUNDATION

## BlueCollar AI — AI Employee SaaS for Blue-Collar Businesses

You are working as a senior full-stack engineer and software architect.

Build ONLY **M1 — Project Foundation** for a production-oriented SaaS called **BlueCollar AI**.

The product will eventually provide AI employees for US blue-collar/home-service businesses, starting with HVAC companies.

IMPORTANT:
M1 is ONLY the technical foundation.

Do NOT implement future product features yet.

# 1. PRODUCT CONTEXT

BlueCollar AI will eventually allow a home-service business to:

* create an account
* onboard its business
* configure services and business hours
* receive customer phone calls
* have an AI receptionist answer calls
* capture customers and leads
* qualify leads
* check appointment availability
* book appointments
* send SMS confirmations and reminders
* maintain call history and transcripts
* configure an AI employee
* use business knowledge
* view operations through a SaaS dashboard

However, NONE of these business features should be implemented in M1.

M1 only establishes the foundation required to build them safely in later phases.
