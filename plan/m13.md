### Ek important production point

M13 ke baad **AI ke paas actual scheduling authority aa jayegi**, lekin authority controlled hai:

**AI decides *what it wants to do*. Backend decides *whether it is allowed to happen*.**

For example, AI bol sakta hai:

> `create_appointment(2 PM)`

Lekin backend check karega:

```text
Is customer valid?
        ↓
Is customer same business ka hai?
        ↓
Lead valid hai?
        ↓
Customer ne confirmation diya?
        ↓
Business us waqt open hai?
        ↓
Minimum notice satisfy hai?
        ↓
Slot abhi bhi available hai?
        ↓
Koi conflicting appointment nahi?
        ↓
Idempotency okay?
        ↓
BOOK
```

Tabhi customer ko confirmation milega.

**M14 ko ab Appointment ke baad communication layer rakhna logical hoga:** appointment confirmation, SMS, reminders, follow-up, missed-call SMS, reschedule/cancellation notifications aur communication preferences. Isliye M13 mein sirf **events/hooks** banaye gaye hain; actual automated SMS engine M14 mein rahega.
