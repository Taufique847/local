### M9 me iska exact meaning

**1. Every phone number belongs to one `businessId`**

```text
BusinessPhoneNumber
{
  businessId,
  phoneNumber,
  phoneNumberSid,
  provider: "twilio",
  isPrimary,
  status
}
```

**2. Incoming call se business identify hoga**

```text
Customer calls
      ↓
Twilio "To" = +13125551001
      ↓
Find BusinessPhoneNumber
      ↓
businessId = ABC Heating
      ↓
Create CallLog for ABC Heating
```

**3. Business A kabhi Business B ka data nahi dekh sakta**

Backend queries hamesha effectively:

```text
WHERE businessId = authenticatedBusinessId
```

Phone APIs, CallLogs, numbers, settings — **sab business-scoped** hone chahiye.

**4. Frontend se `businessId` trust nahi karna**

Bad:

```js
GET /api/calls?businessId=businessB
```

Good:

```text
Authenticated user
      ↓
User → Business
      ↓
Backend determines businessId
      ↓
Query only that business
```

**5. Twilio webhook me JWT nahi hoga**

Twilio incoming webhook normal logged-in browser request nahi hai.

Isliye:

```text
Twilio webhook
      ↓
Validate Twilio signature
      ↓
"To" phone number
      ↓
BusinessPhoneNumber
      ↓
Business
```

Yahi M9 ka most important routing mechanism hai.

### Ek important distinction

Agar tum **one global Twilio account** use kar rahe ho:

```text
BlueCollar AI Twilio Account
├── Business A number
├── Business B number
└── Business C number
```

to multi-tenancy primarily **your database/business isolation layer** handle karegi.

Later agar har customer apna **separate Twilio account** connect kare, architecture aur advanced ho jayega:

```text

Business B → Twilio Account B

```

**M9 MVP ke liye global Twilio account + business-scoped phone numbers** simpler architecture hai. Future me per-business Twilio accounts support karne ke liye provider configuration abstraction rakhna useful hai.
