# M3 — BUSINESS ONBOARDING

The goal is to allow an authenticated user to create and configure their home-service business.

BlueCollar AI is a B2B SaaS platform for US blue-collar/home-service businesses, initially targeting HVAC companies.

M3 should create the foundation for the future relationship:

User
↓
Business
↓
Business Configuration
↓
AI Employee
↓
Customers / Leads / Appointments

Only implement the Business + Onboarding layer in M3.

==================================================

# 1. M3 OBJECTIVE

==================================================

At the end of M3:

A newly registered user should:

1. Login.
2. Enter the application.
3. If no business exists, be sent to onboarding.
4. Create their business.
5. Enter business information.
6. Configure services.
7. Configure service area.
8. Configure business hours.
9. Configure emergency availability.
10. Review the information.
11. Complete onboarding.
12. Be redirected to the application home/dashboard placeholder.

Existing users who already completed onboarding should NOT be forced through onboarding again.
